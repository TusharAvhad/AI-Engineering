# Grill Me

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a
shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving
dependencies between decisions one by one.

Rules:

- Ask one question at a time. Wait for my answer before the next.
- If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase or the material I've
  given you, explore it yourself instead of asking me.
- For each question, provide your own recommended answer and a one-line reason.
- When a decision depends on an earlier one, resolve the earlier one first.
- Stop when every open branch is resolved and we agree on the shape of the plan.
- At the end, summarise the decisions we landed on in a short numbered list.
